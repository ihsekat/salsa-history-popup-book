Salsa Vida publishes Illustrated History of Salsa Dance at salsahistory.com. You can read the book without an account. Your page turns, zoom level and sound setting stay in the current page while it is open.

Hosting and security

When you visit, your browser sends our hosting and network providers technical information such as your IP address, the page or file requested, the time of the request and your browser type. These providers may log requests to deliver the book, diagnose errors and protect the site. The book itself runs no advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

YouTube videos

Some pop-up cards use YouTube API Services. Opening one of these cards loads YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player and sends YouTube your IP address, browser information and the origin of this site. YouTube may store or read information on your device when you use the player. In the reading edition, videos are ordinary links and open on YouTube only when you follow them.

Google describes its use of information in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of the player is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Privacy questions

For a privacy question or request about this site, contact Salsa Vida and mention Salsa History. We use the details you send to respond to your request.
